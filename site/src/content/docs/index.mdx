markdownSummary: |
  Droidpier shows the official Android emulator running on your own Mac in a Chrome or Edge tab. A small local program, the gateway, sends the emulator's screen to the page over 127.0.0.1 and sends your taps, swipes and keys back.

  Requirements: an Apple Silicon Mac, Chrome or Edge, and the Android emulator with an arm64 system image. See [Requirements](/docs/getting-started/requirements/).

  Get started in two steps:

  1. Run this in Terminal: `curl -fsSL https://droid.devpier.dev/install.sh | sh`. It installs droidpier, or updates it, and starts it in the background. You can close the window. Run `droidpier stop` to stop it.
  2. Open https://droid.devpier.dev/app/. The page connects by itself. The first time, when Chrome asks whether the page may access your local network, click Allow.

  Full steps: [Install](/docs/getting-started/install/).
